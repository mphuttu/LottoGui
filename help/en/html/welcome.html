LottoGui – Help

LottoGui is a Windows-based lottery assistant that analyses historical CSV draw data and generates statistically weighted suggestion rows for seven Finnish and Nordic lottery games, as well as a standalone classic lottery row generator.

Key features

  • Statistics-based number prediction (1–50 suggestion rows)
  • Comprehensive per-column statistical analysis (mean, median, mode, quartiles, etc.)
  • Classic lottery row generator with a custom range or your own numbers
  • Check prediction rows against drawn main and bonus numbers, with game-specific result labels
  • Save results to file and print

Getting started

  1. Launch the LottoGui application.
  2. Choose the desired game from the Lotto Options menu.
  3. Configure the settings in the dialog that opens and confirm with OK.
  4. Prediction rows and statistics appear in the main window.
  5. Save or print results using the File menu commands.
Note: CSV data files must be stored in the data\ subfolder of the application's installation directory. The program cannot generate predictions without up-to-date historical data.

Lottery strategies: frequently asked questions

Do strategies improve the odds?

Every valid individual row has the same chance under the game rules. Historical frequencies, balanced numbers and other selection strategies do not improve that chance. Less popular numbers may reduce prize sharing but do not make a row more likely to win.

What are the jackpot odds?

These are per basic row and draw, without add-on games, not the odds of winning any prize. Verify current rules with Veikkaus.

In Keno the chance of matching every selected number depends on level: at level k it is C(20, k) / C(70, k), where C denotes a combination count. In All or Nothing, 12 and 0 matches are separate equally likely jackpot outcomes; each has a chance of 1 / 2,704,156.

Quick pick or personal numbers?

The odds for a single row are identical. Personal selection can avoid popular choices or repeat a row, but repeating it does not make the next draw more likely to win and does not require playing every draw.

When should I buy and which game should I choose?

Purchase timing does not change the chance of a match. A large jackpot can change expected value, but sharing, other prizes and ticket cost matter; a large jackpot alone does not guarantee positive expected value. Compare prize sizes and costs as well as jackpot odds.

What about multiple rows or group play?

More distinct rows cover more combinations but cost more. A group shares costs and any winnings. Do not feel obliged to reinvest wins or chase losses: no method or individual success story guarantees a win.

Play responsibly

Treat lottery play as entertainment, not investment. Set a budget and stick to it; play only with money you can afford to lose. For support in Finland, visit Peluuri.
